Power Tuning, S4 Hibernation and TDP Limits on MSI Claw

S0 Modern Standby hazards, switching to S4 hibernation, the PL2 = PL1 + 1W rule, 3 TDP scenarios, and unlocking fans past 75%.

Hardware: MSI Claw 8 AI+, Claw 7 AI+, Claw A1M
Platform: Intel Core Ultra (Lunar Lake, Meteor Lake)
Reading Time: 6 minutes

1. The S0 Modern Standby Problem

By default, Windows 11 uses S0 Low Power Idle (Modern Standby): the SoC and Wi-Fi stay powered for background updates and telemetry.

Hazards of S0 on a Handheld:
  • Thermal trapping in cases: background wake triggers spin up the SoC inside a sealed carry case, heating to 90-95°C and damaging the battery.
  • Overnight drain: continuous background wakeups lose 25%..100% charge overnight.

2. Switching to S4 Hibernation

In S4 Hibernation, Windows writes RAM to hiberfil.sys on the NVMe SSD and cuts all board power.

Metric S0 Modern Standby (Stock) S4 Hibernation (Recommended)
Idle Battery Drain 3-8% per hour 0% (full power-off)
Bag Overheat Risk High (wake triggers) Eliminated
Suspended Game State May crash 100% RAM preserved
Resume Time 1-2 seconds 6-9 seconds via PCIe 4.0 SSD
One-Click Setup: the Power module in Optimize-Handheld configures S4 automatically (S0 timeout = 0, auto-hibernate 10 min battery / 60 min AC, power buttons to hibernate, wake timers disabled).

Manual Setup (Elevated PowerShell):

powercfg /hibernate on
powercfg /h /type full

Then open Win + R → control powercfg.cpl → "Choose what the power buttons do" → "Change settings that are currently unavailable" → set Hibernate for On battery and Plugged in, and save.

3. TDP (PL1/PL2) and CPU Boost Tuning via Overlay

Keep the PL2 Switch Off: off by default in ClawTweaks (PL2 = PL1 + 1W), eliminating 37W power spikes and sudden fan ramps. Target GPU clocks in overlay: 1950 MHz on Lunar Lake (Claw 8 AI+) and 2250 MHz on Meteor Lake (Claw A1M).

Three Overlay Scenarios (Right Claw Button):

  • 1. GPU-Bound (99:100% GPU load, flat 1950 MHz): disable CPU Turbo Boost; if shader loads and frametimes stay smooth, keep it off permanently.
  • 2. CPU Starves GPU (GPU <95% or dips below 1950 MHz): disable CPU Turbo Boost so power flows back to hold 1950 MHz; on battery, set PL1 1W below observed peak draw.
  • 3. CPU-Heavy Titles (strategies, emulators, Ubisoft Anvil): enable CPU Turbo Boost, turn the PL2 switch On (+3..5W over PL1), and raise limits (recommended on AC power).

Practical Power Targets:

  • Typical 3D Gaming: PL1 = 20W, PL2 = 21W with CPU boost off keeps Arc 140V at 1950 MHz cleanly.
  • FPS Cap Over Watt Clamping: capping at 40/45/60 FPS in ClawTweaks scales wattage down automatically while preserving transient headroom.
  • Max Endurance (2D, visual novels, retro): PL1 = 8W, PL2 = 9W + FPS cap.
Docked Play & 65W Charger Limit: the stock 65W charger covers the handheld alone. Through a USB-C dock with an external display and 30:35W TDP, the battery discharges; use a 100W USB Power Delivery adapter.

4. Fan Curves: Removing the 75% Ceiling

The EC controller defines a 6-point curve (0°C, 50°C, 60°C, 70°C, 80°C, 88°C), where stock firmware clamps the top two points at 67% and 75% (causing 85:90°C heat soak and throttling above 20W).

Unlocking in ClawTweaks: scroll down in the Fan tab, toggle Extended range >75, raise 80°C and 88°C to 85% and 120%, and click Apply Fan Curve.

Verified Quiet Fan Curve (for MSI Center M User Scenario → Fan Speed → Advanced or ClawTweaks under light loads): 

5%, 5%, 25%, 65%, 75%, 100%

Coil Whine Mitigation: if inductor whine appears under shifting loads, run maximum fans (Cooler Boost or 100-150%) for 5 minutes to equalize choke temperatures.
